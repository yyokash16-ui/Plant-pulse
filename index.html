How to interpret this equipment

Suction pressure: pressure at the pump inlet. Low values may indicate a supply restriction or low source pressure.

Discharge pressure: pressure at the outlet. Compare with system conditions and pump curve.

Flow rate: delivered volume per hour. Low flow may relate to process demand, restrictions or instrument issues.

Vibration and bearing temperature: condition indicators that need trend and site-specific alarm limits; neither alone confirms root cause.
